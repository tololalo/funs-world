FEATURES

Why FunS Talk?

No email, no phone number. Your conversations stay yours — and you can send crypto without leaving the chat.

🔐 Full E2E Encryption (Military-Grade)

Only the sender and the receiver can read a message. Military-grade encryption means even our servers can't see the contents.

👛 Sign Up with a Wallet

A wallet is created automatically when you install the app. Log in with a PIN or your fingerprint. Connecting an existing wallet like FunS Wallet or MetaMask works too.

💸 Send Crypto in Chat

Send crypto like BNB right in the middle of a conversation. One /send line is all it takes — no copy-pasting long addresses.

🎫 Token-Gated ChannelsSOON

Premium channels open only to holders of specific NFTs or FunS tokens. Build a community that's truly yours.

⛽ Zero Gas Fee Worries

Chatting is completely free. Wallet-to-wallet transfers require a small gas fee, but you can just use it like WhatsApp or any other messenger.

👥 Group Chat & File SharingSOON

Share files and media safely in encrypted group chats — with the same level of security as your 1:1 conversations.

HOW TO USE

Getting started is really simple

As easy as any messenger you already use. The blockchain runs quietly in the background.

1Install the App

Download the app and your wallet is created automatically. No extra setup needed.

2PIN / Biometrics

Log in with a fingerprint or a PIN. No seed phrase to memorize.

3Add Friends & Chat

Add friends by wallet address. Every conversation is encrypted automatically.

4Send & Community

Send crypto right in the chat and join token-gated channels.
